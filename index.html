Welcome!

This is the website of the arithmetic geometry and automorphic forms working group at Zhejiang University. We are researchers from the School of Mathematical Sciences and the Institute of Advanced Study in Mathematics, pursuing questions related to automorphic and modular forms, representations of real and p-adic groups, L-functions, the arithmetic of Shimura varieties, and algebraic geometry over the integers and p-adic numbers.

We usually meet at IASM on Wednesdays at 4 pm for our weekly seminar. See our seminar calendar for dates and more information.
